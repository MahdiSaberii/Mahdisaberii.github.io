abstract: >
 Multi-echo fMRI (ME-fMRI) has the potential to distinguish blood oxygen level dependent (BOLD) signals from confounding factors such as cardiac or respiratory effects that are not echo time (TE) dependent. Non-Cartesian trajectories, such as spirals, have gained interest in ME-fMRI because of their efficient k-space coverage. Achieving high spatio-temporal resolution with spiral readouts requires high acceleration rates, where conventional parallel imaging methods fail. Physics-driven deep learning (PD-DL) methods have shown promise in MR image reconstruction by integrating model-based data fidelity with learned image priors, even in scenarios where fully-sampled data is not available, and have been applied to non-Cartesian fMRI. However, their performance is adversely affected by the image domain phase variations across echoes. In this study, we propose to incorporate the echo image phases explicitly into the PD-DL reconstruction, via a dedicated phase estimation network. Results show substantial image quality over standard PD-DL methods at acceleration rate (R) = 6.
